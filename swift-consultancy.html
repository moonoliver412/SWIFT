Stay Safe

How to spot a fake agent

A genuine agency will never ask you to pay for a "job guarantee" before an interview, rush you to send money to a personal M-Pesa number, or refuse to show you a contract. If anyone does — walk away, and report them to the authorities.

Questions

Before you ask

How much does the process cost?

Costs vary by destination and role. We give you a full written breakdown — registration, medicals, visa, ticket — before you pay anything. You will never be surprised by a fee mid-process.

How long does it take to fly out?

Most placements take 2–4 months from registration to departure, depending on the destination, visa processing times and how quickly your documents are ready.

What documents do I need to register?

A valid passport, your CV, education or training certificates, and a certificate of good conduct. If you're missing something, we'll guide you on how to get it.

Do I need experience to qualify?

Not always. Many roles in hospitality, care work and logistics accept entry-level candidates and provide training. Skilled trades and healthcare roles require certificates and experience.

How do I know Swift is genuine?

Visit our Nairobi office in person, ask for our registration documents, and talk to candidates we've placed. A genuine agency has nothing to hide — and we encourage you to verify before you commit.
