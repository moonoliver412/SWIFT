Questions

Frequently asked questions

Honest answers about costs, timelines, documents and how to verify us. Can't find yours? Ask us directly — we respond within one business day.

How much does the process cost?

Costs vary by destination and role. We give you a full written breakdown — registration, medicals, visa, ticket — before you pay anything. You will never be surprised by a fee mid-process.

How long does it take to fly out?

Most placements take 2–4 months from registration to departure, depending on the destination, visa processing times and how quickly your documents are ready.

What documents do I need to register?

A valid passport, your CV, education or training certificates, and a certificate of good conduct. If you're missing something, we'll guide you on how to get it.

Do I need experience to qualify?

Not always. Many roles in hospitality, care work and logistics accept entry-level candidates and provide training. Skilled trades and healthcare roles require certificates and experience. Check the open jobs board for current requirements.

How do I know Swift is genuine?

Visit our Nairobi office in person, ask for our registration documents, and talk to candidates we've placed. A genuine agency has nothing to hide — and we encourage you to verify before you commit. Read more about how we work.

Still have a question?

Message us on WhatsApp or send the form — we respond within one business day.
