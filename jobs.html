Sectors We Recruit For

Where Kenyan talent is in demand

These are the sectors we place candidates in most often. Entry-level roles exist in hospitality, care work and logistics; skilled trades and healthcare need certificates and experience.

Hospitality — UAE & Qatar

Hotel staff, housekeeping, waiters, kitchen teams and front office for hotels and resorts in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Doha. Many roles accept entry-level candidates with training provided.

Healthcare & care work — UK & Canada

Care assistants, live-in caregivers and registered nurses. These roles require certificates, and UK healthcare roles need sponsorship — we handle the paperwork with you.

Transport & logistics — KSA & Poland

Heavy and light drivers for Saudi Arabia and warehouse operatives for Poland and wider Europe. Valid licences and clean records required for driving roles.
