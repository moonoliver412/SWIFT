What We Do

Everything between you and the job

From the day you register to the day you land — and after. One agency, one fee structure in writing, no middlemen.

Job sourcing

Direct relationships with vetted employers and licensed partner agencies abroad — we find the role that fits your skills. See our current open jobs.

Visa processing

Work visas, permits and embassy appointments handled end-to-end, with honest timelines and zero guesswork.

Document attestation

Certificate verification, good conduct, medicals and attestation done correctly the first time — no rejected files.

Contract review

We go through your salary, hours, accommodation and terms with you in plain language before you commit.

Pre-departure training

Culture, workplace expectations, money and rights abroad — so the first month doesn't catch you off guard.

Post-arrival support

A contact on the ground and a direct line back to Nairobi. We stay reachable after you land — not just until you pay.

The Journey

From application to boarding gate

A clear, four-stage route. You'll always know exactly where you are and what comes next.

01

Register & verify

Submit your CV, passport and certificates. We verify your documents and build your candidate profile.

02

Get matched

We match your profile to a verified employer abroad and confirm your job offer in writing.

03

Visa & contract

We process your work visa, permits and medicals, and review your contract with you line by line before you sign.

✈

Fly out

Pre-departure briefing, ticketing and airport support. You land with a job, a contract and a contact on the ground.
