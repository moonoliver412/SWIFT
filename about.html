Why Swift

Migration done the right way

Too many Kenyans have been burned by fake agents and ghost jobs. Swift was built to be the opposite: registered, transparent and accountable at every step.

Our Promise

What working with Swift means

From your first office visit to your first month abroad, you'll always know what is happening, what it costs, and who is responsible.

  • Registered Kenyan agency — verify us before you pay anything
  • Every employer and contract is vetted before we present it to you
  • Transparent fee structure in writing — no hidden charges mid-process
  • You see and understand your contract before you sign it
Stay Safe

How to spot a fake agent

A genuine agency will never ask you to pay for a "job guarantee" before you have a written offer, rush you to send money to a personal M-Pesa number, or refuse to show you a contract. If anyone does — walk away, and report them to the authorities.
